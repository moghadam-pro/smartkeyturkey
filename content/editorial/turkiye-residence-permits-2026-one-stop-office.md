Türkiye opened a new One-Stop Office at the Istanbul Financial Center in May 2026. The initiative is relevant to international investors because it brings several public services into one coordinated location and aims to accelerate selected work- and residence-related procedures.

It is also easy to misunderstand. The One-Stop Office is not a new general residence permit, and it does not mean that every foreign national can complete every immigration procedure there. It is a service model focused on foreign investors, company employees and their family members within the Istanbul Financial Center framework.

## What services are brought together?

The Presidency of Migration Management says the office was established under the coordination of the Presidency’s Investment and Finance Office. Units representing the Ministry of Interior, Ministry of Labour and Social Security, Ministry of Treasury and Finance, Ministry of Trade and Ümraniye Municipality provide services there.

For eligible foreign investors, employees and family members, Migration Management services include:

- residence-permit procedures;
- address registration;
- allocation of foreign identity numbers;
- fingerprints and biometric registration.

The official announcement says the model is intended to speed up administrative processes—particularly work and residence permits—and make public services easier to access. Digital processes are handled through the Istanbul Financial Center portal.

## Who should not assume the office applies to them?

A property buyer, tourist, student or remote worker should not assume that the Istanbul Financial Center office replaces the standard e-Residence process. The applicable route depends on the purpose of stay, the applicant’s status, the sponsoring entity and the location handling the file.

Applicants outside the relevant investor or company framework should continue to use the official channels identified by the Presidency of Migration Management. Do not pay an intermediary merely because they claim to have “One-Stop Office access.” Confirm eligibility and the correct application channel first.

## Residence through property is a separate route

Invest in Türkiye’s official property guide states that foreign nationals do not need a residence permit as a precondition to buy real estate. It also says foreigners who acquire property may apply for renewable short-term residence permits under Law No. 6458.

That does not mean every purchase guarantees approval. The property, title record, intended use, applicant documentation and current administrative criteria still require checking. Citizenship by investment is another distinct process: the official guide currently identifies a minimum qualifying real-estate value of USD 400,000, with a three-year restriction on resale and confirmation by the competent authority.

SmartKeyTurkey does not publish a property as “residence guaranteed” or “citizenship guaranteed.” The property transaction and the immigration application must each pass their own official review.

## A practical 2026 preparation checklist

The official Invest in Türkiye residence guide lists core documents commonly relevant to an initial short-term application, including:

- the residence permit application form;
- a passport or equivalent travel document;
- biometric photographs;
- a declaration of sufficient and regular financial means;
- receipts for the residence-permit and document fees;
- valid health insurance;
- title-deed evidence when the application is based on property ownership.

Additional documentation may apply to business connections, company activity, family members or investment-based categories. Applicants should use the current checklist generated for their own application and should not rely on an old document list copied from a blog or social-media post.

## Official 2026 fees

Migration Management lists the 2026 residence-permit document fee as TRY 964. The separate residence-permit fee can depend on nationality, reciprocity, permit duration and applicable exemptions. The authority publishes country-specific and general fee information, so a single figure should not be presented as the total cost for every applicant.

Budget separately for translations, notarization, health insurance, photographs and professional advice where required. Never transfer government fees to an unverified personal account.

## Timing and extensions

The official residence guide says extension applications may be made during the 60 days before the existing permit expires and must not be left until after the expiry date. The guide also describes document-submission requirements following an e-Residence extension application.

Because procedures can change, applicants should check the current e-Residence instructions and any notice issued by the responsible provincial authority. Screenshots and informal advice from a previous applicant may no longer reflect the current workflow.

## How investors should organize the process

For a coordinated move, create four separate files:

1. **Investment or employment:** company, role, capital and supporting corporate documents.
2. **Property:** title, valuation, restrictions, debts, insurance and intended use.
3. **Immigration:** application category, appointment, passport, insurance and family documents.
4. **Tax and banking:** residence analysis, source of funds and ongoing reporting obligations.

The One-Stop Office may reduce friction for eligible investors and employees, but good preparation remains essential. A centralized counter cannot correct an unsuitable application category, an incomplete title review or inconsistent personal information.

## The bottom line

The Istanbul Financial Center One-Stop Office is a meaningful service improvement for the international investment ecosystem. Its value lies in coordination—not in creating automatic approvals. Investors should confirm whether they fall within its scope, prepare the correct application route and verify every time-sensitive requirement through official sources.
